# A little room to write

A simple place for **clear thoughts** and good ideas.

## Try it

- [ ] Edit this text on the left and watch the preview on the right.
- [ ] Select a word and press **Ctrl+B** to make it bold.
- [ ] Press **Ctrl+F** to find and replace text.
- [ ] Try **Write**, **Split**, and **Read** at the top.
- [ ] Save your own copy with **Ctrl+Shift+S**.

> Your notes stay in ordinary Markdown files on your computer.

> [!NOTE]
> Click a checkbox in the preview to update the source, then save with **Ctrl+S**.

> [!TIP]
> Use **Ctrl+Z** in the editor to undo a checkbox change.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Save your changes before moving on.

> [!WARNING]
> Files changed outside MarkEdit require confirmation before overwriting.

> [!CAUTION]
> This app uses explicit saves, without automatic crash recovery.
